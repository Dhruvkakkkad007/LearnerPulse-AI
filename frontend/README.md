# 🎓 LearnAI - Student Learner Type & Performance Prediction System
### *Super Simple Step-by-Step Guide* 🧒✨

---

## 🌟 What Is This Project?
Think of this project as a **Magic School Robot** 🤖 that analyzes how you study, how you use AI, and how you sleep, to tell you:
1. **Will you pass your exams?** 🎯
2. **What kind of student superhero are you?** 🦸‍♂️
3. **What secret habits can make you get top grades?** 🚀

---

## 🧠 1. AI Predictor (The Magic Fortune Teller)
* **What it does**: You input your daily study details (hours, exam scores, AI tools used, sleep, attendance). When you press the **"Test Predictor"** button, it calculates your probability of passing, assigns your grade tier, and writes a personalized study roadmap!
* **How it works**: Uses an optimized **Decision Tree** (trained on 8,000 students) that asks smart sequential questions (e.g. *Did you understand the core concepts? Are you overly dependent on AI?*).
* **What you get**:
  - 🟢 Pass/Fail Probability Gauge (`%`)
  - 🏆 Performance Tier (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`)
  - 📊 6-Metric Cognitive & Habit Radar Chart
  - 🗺️ Step-by-step personalized action plan (Feynman Technique, sleep fixes).

---

## 📊 2. Model Analytics (The Science Lab)
* **What it does**: Shows all the proof and charts from the **8,000 real students** dataset.
* **What it has**:
  - 🥇 **Feature Importance**: Proves that *Concept Understanding (38.4%)* and *Consistency (17.5%)* matter way more than just raw study hours.
  - 🥣 **Depth vs Accuracy (Goldilocks Curve)**: Shows why Depth 4 gives the cleanest 94.8% accuracy without overfitting.
  - 🎯 **Confusion Matrix**: Shows that 98 out of 100 predictions are 100% accurate.

---

## 🧬 3. Learner Personas (The 5 Student Characters)
Every student has a learning personality:
1. 🤖 **AI-Augmented Innovator**: Uses AI to learn faster, but always understands the concept.
2. 📚 **Disciplined Deep Scholar**: Loves books, writes notes by hand, great memory.
3. ⚖️ **Balanced Strategic Achiever**: Healthy sleep, steady grades, good life balance.
4. ⚠️ **High-Risk AI Dependent**: Copies everything without understanding; gets trapped in real exams.
5. 🌙 **Social Nocturnal Crammer**: Late-night phone scroller who crams before deadlines.

---

## 🚀 4. AI Study Planner & Grade Booster (Your A+ Coach)
* **What it does**: Takes your current score, target grade (e.g. `88% A+`), and exam countdown, and generates a **customized phase-by-phase 7-day study timetable**.
* **Features**:
  - 🎯 **Target Gap Calculator**: Shows the exact score boost needed.
  - 📅 **Structured Weekly Roadmap**: Days 1-2 (Concepts), Days 3-4 (AI Quizzes), Days 5-6 (Past Paper Mocks), Final Day (Sleep Recharge).
  - 🔥 **Interactive Daily Habit Streak Tracker**: Checkable daily tasks (Feynman recall, screen-free drills).
  - 🖨️ **Printable Timetable**: One-click print or save to PDF!
